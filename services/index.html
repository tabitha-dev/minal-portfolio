Services

Support that meets you where you are.

Psychological support does not have to begin with a diagnosis or a crisis. It can begin with a question, a pattern you want to understand, a difficult season, or a desire to feel more grounded in your life.

01

Individuals

A space to explore stress, emotional overwhelm, anxiety, low mood, self-esteem, fears, transitions, emotional regulation and personal growth in the context of your life.

02

Couples

Support for relationship concerns, communication difficulties, repeated patterns, emotional distance and the work of understanding one another more clearly.

03

Families

A supportive setting to look at family dynamics, communication, transitions and the ways people affect and respond to one another within the family system.

04

Students

Support for academic concerns, adjustment difficulties, confidence, emotional well-being, decision-making, study-related stress and questions about career direction.

05

Working professionals

A practical, psychologically informed space for work stress, burnout, career uncertainty, emotional regulation, interpersonal challenges and healthier work-life boundaries.

Online enquiries & appointment details

You can begin with a brief online enquiry. Any consultation format, scope, availability, fees and practical arrangements will be clarified before a booking is confirmed. No in-person office is currently advertised.

What the first conversation can be

The first conversation does not need to solve everything. It can be a chance to describe what has been happening, ask questions, understand whether the support offered here fits your needs and discuss what a useful next step could look like.

Prefer a low-pressure first step?

You can contact Minal first rather than booking immediately.
