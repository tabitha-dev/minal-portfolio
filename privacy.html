Website notice

Privacy, information & scope.

Information about this website, the current contact method and important boundaries. Privacy and professional consent policies still require Minal’s approval before launch.

General information

Information on this website is educational and general in nature. It does not create a psychologist-client relationship, provide a diagnosis or replace individualized psychological, psychiatric or medical care.

Website enquiries

This is a static website. The enquiry form opens the visitor's own email application and does not send or store the message on this website. Email messages are not inherently a secure way to transmit health information; avoid including confidential or sensitive clinical details in your first enquiry.

Appointments and third-party services

No appointment-booking provider is connected in this version. If one is added, its privacy practices and terms should be reviewed before launch. Contact directly to confirm the consultation format and policies.

Emergencies

This website is not an emergency or crisis service. In India, call 112 for immediate danger. The Government of India's Tele-MANAS mental-health helpline is available at 14416.

Professional information

Public professional titles, credentials, registrations, scope of services and locations served should be confirmed by Minal before launch and kept current.
