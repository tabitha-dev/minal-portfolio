HR & Workplace

Work is part of life, not separate from it.

Minal's earlier HR experience adds a practical perspective to work-related stress, people dynamics, career questions and the emotional realities of professional life.

A psychology + workplace perspective

Before focusing on psychology, Minal worked as an HR Executive for two years, supporting recruitment, onboarding, employee coordination and other HR responsibilities. That experience informs an understanding of how workplace systems, expectations and relationships can affect well-being.

For working professionals

Individual support may include conversations about work-related stress, burnout, confidence, communication, career direction, boundaries, transitions, interpersonal difficulties and the challenge of balancing professional demands with the rest of life.

Looking ahead

This part of the website is intentionally designed to grow. Future offerings may include workplace well-being education, psychoeducation, workshops or organizational collaboration once Minal defines the scope, format and professional requirements for those services.

Current focus

Individual psychological support for working professionals is part of Minal's present service direction. Organizational consulting or workshops are shown here as a future growth area, not as a current claim.
