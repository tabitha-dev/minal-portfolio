Website notice

Privacy, information & scope.

A concise placeholder notice for the pre-launch website. This should be professionally reviewed and adapted to the final booking provider, hosting setup, analytics tools and applicable laws before publication.

General information

Information on this website is educational and general in nature. It does not create a psychologist-client relationship, provide a diagnosis or replace individualized psychological, psychiatric or medical care.

Website enquiries

The current contact form is a static mailto form: it does not store information on the website and opens the visitor's own email application. Visitors should avoid including highly sensitive or detailed clinical information in an initial website enquiry.

Appointments and third-party services

The final site will link to a separate appointment-booking service. That provider may have its own privacy policy, data practices and terms. Those details should be reviewed before the booking link is added.

Emergencies

This website is not an emergency or crisis service. In India, call 112 for immediate danger. The Government of India's Tele-MANAS mental-health helpline is available at 14416.

Professional information

Public professional titles, credentials, registrations, scope of services and locations served should be confirmed by Minal before launch and kept current.
