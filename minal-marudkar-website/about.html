I believe every person has a story that deserves to be heard. My work is grounded in listening carefully, understanding context and creating room for people to speak without fear of judgment.

A safe space to be heard.

I am Minal Marudkar, a psychologist, author and HR professional. My background in Clinical Psychology, workplace experience and interest in mind-body well-being shape a practical, people-centered perspective.

Life can bring stress, anxiety, relationship difficulties, emotional struggles, self-doubt and transitions that are difficult to navigate alone. Many people hesitate to seek support because they fear judgment or feel they should manage everything themselves. I want to help make that first step feel more normal and more human.

My goal is to offer a calm, empathetic and non-judgmental space where you can speak openly, understand yourself more clearly and consider meaningful next steps toward emotional well-being.

Mental health deserves attention before a small concern becomes a bigger one.

Professional background

My training includes an M.A. in Psychology (Clinical), a diploma in basic counselling skills and focused workshops in Cognitive Behaviour Therapy, grief counselling, mental status examination, mind-body connection and psychological testing. My earlier professional experience in Human Resources adds a practical understanding of work, people, communication and organizational life.

My approach

I value active listening, rapport, behavioural observation, psychoeducation and thoughtful use of evidence-informed psychological approaches. I also believe support should be understandable: clear language, no unnecessary jargon and space for questions.

Vision & mission

My vision is to contribute to greater mental-health awareness and to help make psychological well-being a priority in everyday life. My mission is to educate, encourage early support and empower people to take care of their mental and emotional well-being with the same seriousness given to physical health.
